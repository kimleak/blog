# Audience

## Who reads this blog

**Primary reader:** Curious, tech-aware people who aren't developers.
They use apps, follow tech news, and want to understand AI —
but they don't write code and don't want to feel dumb.

**Age range:** 20–40 years old
**Background:** Students, creatives, marketers, entrepreneurs
**Tech level:** Comfortable with smartphones and apps. Not comfortable with code.

---

## What they already know
- They've heard of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
- They know AI is a big deal but aren't sure exactly why
- They use Google, YouTube, Instagram every day
- They've seen tech headlines but often find them confusing

## What they want to learn
- What's actually happening in AI — explained simply
- How new tech affects their daily life
- Whether they should care about a new tool or trend
- Practical things they can do or try right now

## What they hate
- Articles that make them feel stupid
- Too much jargon without explanation
- Hype without substance ("this will change everything!")
- Long intros that bury the point

---

## The promise of this blog
Every article should leave the reader thinking:
**"Oh, I actually get that now — and I know what to do with it."**
