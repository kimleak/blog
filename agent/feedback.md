## How to write feedback

Just be honest and specific. For example:

**What I liked:**
- The analogy in the third paragraph was perfect
- The hook grabbed me right away

**What to change next time:**
- The ending felt rushed — give it more space
- Don't use the word "basically" — I use it too much already
- The middle section was a bit long, trim it
